Meet your dentist

Dentistry that explains first and treats second.

Dr. Ananya Rao is a prosthodontist with over a decade of experience in restorative and cosmetic dentistry. She believes the best treatment is the most conservative one that works, so every plan starts with a clear conversation, photographs and X-rays where needed, and a written estimate you can take home.

  • Single-use needles, instruments sterilised in a Class B autoclave
  • Digital X-rays with lead-lined protection and minimal radiation
  • Written treatment plan and cost estimate before any procedure
  • Comfort first: topical anaesthesia, breaks on request, no judgement

Your first visit

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01

    Listen & examine

    We start with your history and concerns, then a full check of teeth, gums, bite and soft tissues.

  2. 02

    Scan & diagnose

    Low-dose digital X-rays and photos where clinically needed. We show you exactly what we see.

  3. 03

    Plan & quote

    Options explained in plain language, ranked by urgency, with a written estimate. No pressure.

  4. 04

    Treat & maintain

    Gentle treatment, clear aftercare, and a recall schedule that suits your risk level.
